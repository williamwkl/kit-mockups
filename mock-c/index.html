    Packing guides

    Four common trips, and which KIT pieces solve them.

    Guide 01

    Packing for a weekend trip?

    Two or three days, one bag. One packing cube for clothes, one pouch for chargers and small items, one shoe bag so nothing touches your clean clothes. The Demetri printed cube set has mesh panels so you can see what is inside without unzipping.

    See the packing list

    Guide 02

    Flying with toiletries in your carry-on?

    Keep toiletries and personal care items together in one bag you can pull out at security. The Garrison has a fold-open design and a built-in hanger, so it works in the hotel bathroom too; the Fedora and Finn pouches hold makeup and small personal care items.     Tired of digging through your everyday bag?

    Give each kind of thing its own pouch: one for cards and receipts, one for makeup, one for cables. The Finn halfmoon pouch is small enough for a handbag; the Caprice mesh envelopes let you see documents at a glance.
